# ADR-0019: Explicit incomplete and abstained terminal states

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-09
- Source: [Proposed ADR S](../planning-pack/18_INITIAL_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md)

## Decision

Uncertainty and insufficient evidence are typed outcomes.

## Reason

Reliable systems must not fabricate success.

## Consequence

Callers and UIs handle non-success states explicitly.
